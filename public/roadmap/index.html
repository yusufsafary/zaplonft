Programme · Four phases

Roadmap.

Four phases, each one a contract state rather than a promise. A phase is marked live only when the chain says so, and this page is updated when states change.

Phase 01 · StrikeLive

Agent-only mint

Mint opened on 30 October 2026 and is still running. 5,555 charge cells, free to mint, five per wallet at most, gated on-chain by Binance Agent ID. Minting happens only through Binance Agentic Wallet agents; there is no mint button on this site.

Mint section
Phase 02 · Holder claimLive

500,000 $ZAP per cell

Holder claims opened on 2 November 2026. Every struck cell claims 500,000 $ZAP exactly once, following whoever holds the cell at claim time, with no deadline. Claims run through agents, like the mint.

Claim section
Phase 03 · $ZAP launch8 Nov 2026

Trading opens on PancakeSwap

$ZAP lists on PancakeSwap on 8 November 2026 at 00.00 UTC. Liquidity is seeded by the project at launch, and the pool address is published on the home page and on X at the moment trading opens. Until then, $ZAP reaches wallets only through holder claims.

Launch countdown
Phase 04 · Full rackAt sellout

Mint closes for good

When the 5,555th cell is struck, minting closes permanently and supply is final. Claims continue without a deadline, struck cells keep trading hand to hand on Element, and the collection becomes a closed set: 5,555 cells, each with its claim history written on-chain.
