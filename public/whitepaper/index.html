Zaplo in one document: a fixed set of 5,555 charge cells minted only by AI agents, a fixed supply of $ZAP that reaches the market through holder claims, and a public launch on PancakeSwap. Every number below can be checked on-chain.

01 · Overview

Built for agents

Zaplo is a collection of 5,555 charge cells on BNB Smart Chain. Minting and claiming are performed exclusively by Binance Agentic Wallets: software agents that hold a Binance Agent ID and sign on their owner's behalf. Humans instruct their agents; agents call the contracts; people confirm in their wallets. This website is read-only and never connects to a wallet, holds keys, or moves funds.

03 · Token

$ZAP, fixed at ten billion

$ZAP is the collection's token. Its whole supply was minted once, at deployment, to the project wallet; the contract has no function to create more. No presale, private round, or team sale has taken place: before launch, $ZAP leaves the project only through holder claims.

04 · Claim mechanics

One claim per cell

Each struck cell claims 500,000 $ZAP exactly once. Claims attach to the tokenId, not to a wallet: if a cell changes hands before claiming, its new holder claims; if it already claimed under a previous owner, nothing remains to claim. A claim pays a small anti-bot fee, read fresh from the claim contract at claim time, and there is no deadline. Tokens sitting in the claim pool cannot be withdrawn by anyone, including the contract owner; only collected fees can be withdrawn.

05 · Launch

PancakeSwap launch

$ZAP does not trade anywhere yet. Public trading opens on PancakeSwap in a ZAP/BNB pool created and seeded with liquidity by the project at launch; the launch date and the pool address will be published on zaplo.nl and on X (@zaplonl) before trading opens. Any pool trading before that official announcement is not the project's pool. Claims continue in parallel, before and after launch: holding an unclaimed cell remains a standing claim on 500,000 $ZAP from the claim pool.

06 · House rules

Rules that do not change

Minting and claiming run only through agents holding an Agent ID. This site stays read-only: no connect button, no browser mint, no custody. Prices and fees are confirmed inside the wallet, never advertised on the page. Supply figures for both the collection and the token are fixed in their contracts; no owner function can raise them.

07 · Risks

Read before you hold

Zaplo is an experimental on-chain collectible. Smart contracts can contain undiscovered faults; $ZAP will be volatile once trading opens and may lose value entirely; agent wallets are new infrastructure and their failure modes are still being learned. Nothing in this document is financial advice or a promise of returns. Verify every address above on BscScan before interacting with it.
