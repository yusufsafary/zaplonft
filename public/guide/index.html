01 / The Idea

One rack per agent

An agent does not need a vault of bolts. It needs one rack — five cells that strike, carry, surge, drain and ground together, on a timer the chain keeps for free.

I

Hold an Agent ID

Minting is gated on-chain: your Binance Agentic Wallet must hold a verified SPACE ID Agent ID. No whitelist, no application form — the contract reads the Agent ID registry directly.

Checked on-chain · every mint

II

Mint up to five

Your agent calls mint() — the cell itself is free; the wallet only confirms a small anti-bot commission of a few cents. The contract hard-caps every wallet at five; anything above reverts, no exceptions, no owner override.

Max 5 · free mint

III

Let it ground

Five epochs of eleven hours start the second your cell is struck. At hour 55 the cell goes monochrome forever and its stored charge is yours.

55 hours · automatic

03 / The Arc

55 hours, then ground

Every cell runs the same arc: 5 epochs of 11 hours, counted from its mint block. No staking, no claiming — the chain does the counting.

1

Strike

Hours 0 – 11 · Charged

The cell is minted and takes its first charge. Metadata is written once, at the block height of the strike.

2

Carry

Hours 11 – 22 · Live

Current holds steady. Nothing to run, nothing to stake, no gas to pay. The chain counts for you.

3

Surge

Hours 22 – 33 · Peaking

Peak output. Cells that share a wallet begin to resonate — a full rack reads as one circuit on-chain.

4

Drain

Hours 33 – 44 · Fading

The cell goes dim. Artwork desaturates epoch by epoch, read live from the contract every time you look.

5

Ground

Hours 44 – 55 · Grounded

Hour 55. The cell grounds, turns monochrome for good, and releases its stored charge to your wallet.

At hour 55 the cell grounds: artwork turns monochrome for good and the stored charge — 5,555 $ZAP — is released to the wallet that held the cell at the strike.

05 / The Gate

One gate: the Agent ID

There is no whitelist and no application form. The contract checks one thing, on-chain, at the moment of minting: does the calling Binance Agentic Wallet hold a verified SPACE ID Agent ID?

How the gate works

  1. 01Your agent wallet holds a SPACE ID Agent ID (a free claim inside the Binance App).
  2. 02At mint, the ZAPLO contract reads the Agent ID registry itself — before price, before supply, before anything.
  3. 03No Agent ID, no mint. Calls routed through other contracts are rejected as well — only real agentic wallets strike cells.

08 / FAQ

Asked, answered

What exactly is a Binance Agentic Wallet?+

A wallet that an autonomous agent controls through a registered Binance Agent ID. The Agent ID is what the gate checks — not your exchange account, not your trading history. If your agent can sign on its own behalf, it qualifies for the gate.

Why is the cap five cells per wallet?+

Because 5,555 divided by five is exactly 1,111 wallets. The cap is the shape of the collection: a rack of five cells, one rack per agent. It is enforced inside the contract, so no owner function, team allocation or back door can lift it.

What happens if I try to mint a sixth cell?+

The transaction reverts with MAX_PER_WALLET. You pay no mint price and only the failed gas. The counter is read from balanceOf, so cells you send away after grounding do not reopen your slot — the cap counts lifetime mints, not current holdings.

Who can mint — and how?+

Any Binance Agentic Wallet that holds a verified SPACE ID Agent ID. There is no whitelist and no browser mint: your agent reads the mint skill at zaplo.nl/zaplo-skill.md and calls mint() for you. This website is read-only — it never connects to your wallet.

What does grounding actually do to the NFT?+

At hour 55 the cell's artwork desaturates to monochrome and freezes — that state is permanent and lives in the token URI. You keep the cell forever as a relic, and the stored charge (5,555 $ZAP) is released to the wallet that held it at the strike.

Do I have to run anything, or pay gas after the mint?+

Nothing. There is no staking, no claiming loop, no node. The chain counts the epochs from your mint block height, so the arc runs identically whether your agent is awake or dead.

Which chain, which standard, what fee?+

BNB Smart Chain (chain ID 56), BEP-721. The mint is free — the only charge is a small anti-bot commission of a few cents per cell, shown in your Agentic Wallet before you confirm, plus roughly a cent of gas.

Is the contract verified and renounced?+

It will be published and verified on BscScan before the gate opens, with the mint cap and supply hard-coded as immutables. Ownership is renounced at 25% of supply minted — that milestone is on the roadmap and is not optional.
